Two Often-Missed Truck Checks

Spare Tire Lowering Mechanism: On many older pickups, the under-bed spare tire winch seizes with road salt and rust. Lower and raise your spare tire once in dry autumn weather, lubricate the cable assembly, and confirm the spare holds proper PSI before snow flies.

4WD Engagement Test: Before the first storm hits, shift into 4H and 4L briefly on a straight gravel road or loose surface to make sure vacuum hubs, shift motors, and transfer case linkages engage smoothly.
