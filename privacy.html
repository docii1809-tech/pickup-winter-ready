Site Policies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Pickup Winter Ready is a static informational website without user accounts, logins, or a site-operated database.

Information We Collect

Pickup Winter Ready does not require registration and does not collect or store personal information on a server operated by this website. The interactive Winter Gear Finder runs completely inside your browser and does not transmit your answers anywhere.

Local Browser Storage

Our interactive Winter Readiness Checklist saves your checked items solely inside your own browser's localStorage under the key pwr_winter_checklist_v1 so your progress stays intact when you move between pages. You can clear this data at any time by clicking "Reset Checklist" or clearing browser site data.

Static Hosting & Fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser loads pages or Google Fonts stylesheets, standard technical access logs (such as IP address and browser user agent) may be processed by those infrastructure providers according to their respective privacy policies.

Third-Party Affiliate Links

When you click an outbound link to Amazon, eBay, or another retailer, you leave Pickup Winter Ready and enter that retailer's website. Those retailers may set cookies or use tracking parameters to attribute qualifying purchases under their own privacy policies.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
